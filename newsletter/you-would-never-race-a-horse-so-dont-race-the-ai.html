You Would Never Race a Horse, So Don't Race the AI

Nobody loses sleep because a horse can outrun them. Yet many of the learners I mentor lose sleep because AI can out-code them, and they put it in almost the same words every time: "AI can already write this faster than I can. Why would anyone hire me?"

The worry is real, and I take it seriously every time I hear it. What I find amusing is the race they are afraid of losing. The worry is not wrong. The race is. My new post is about why humans never win with their bare hands, and why the skill to learn in the AI era is driving, not running.
