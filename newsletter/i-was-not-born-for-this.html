'I Was Not Born for This' Is True Only as Long as You Believe It

As a mentor and as a manager, the sentence I have heard more than any other is "I was not born for this." For years it was my sentence too. I was sure I was not born to lead, and just as sure I was not born to code.

Both beliefs were 100% right, for exactly as long as I held them. My new post is about why that happens, how both of mine finally broke, and the half that motivational posters leave out: some of the things I am bad at, I am bad at on purpose.
